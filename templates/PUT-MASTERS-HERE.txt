Master HTML files go here, plus bd-govt-logo.png.

These are shared by every office. A correction made here reaches all
offices on the next build — that is the point. Never edit a file in
out/, it is overwritten.

Configurable regions are marked in the HTML:

  <!--@S125-->   … <!--/@S125-->     ধারা ১২৫ block   (own lines)
  <!--@KROY-->   … <!--/@KROY-->     জমি ক্রয়ের সতর্কতা (own lines)
  <!--@S126-->   … <!--/@S126-->     ধারা ১২৬ rates    (inline)
  <!--@ANIK-->   … <!--/@ANIK-->     GRS officer       (inline)
  <!--@APPEAL--> … <!--/@APPEAL-->   GRS appeal        (inline)

To add a new configurable region: wrap it in a marker, add the key to
offices/*.json, and add one line to build.py. The boards themselves do
not otherwise change.
